Dinacharya Launcher: privacy policy

App: Dinacharya Launcher (package app.dinacharya.launcher) · Developer: ASG · Last updated: 30 October 2026

Dinacharya Launcher is an Android app that works as a minimal home screen (a launcher) with no apps on it, shows your daily routine as the home screen itself (through a live wallpaper that comes with the app and draws your routine on the phone), rings alarms for the slots of your day and writes daily reports. This page says what the app does with information, in plain words.

The short version

  • Your routine, your alarm answers, your reports and your phone-use figures stay on your phone.
  • The home screen reads which apps are installed on your phone only to show them to you. That list is never sent anywhere.
  • I (the developer) run no server and have no account system. I cannot see your data.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google and back up, a copy goes to a private space in your own Google Drive.
  • The free version shows small ads served by Google AdMob, and optional video ads you can watch to try Pro features for a while (three videos open all the Pro features for a day). Having Dinacharya Pro (monthly or lifetime) removes them.

Google sign-in and Drive backup

Signing in is optional. When you sign in, the app asks Google for permission to use the app’s own hidden space in your Drive (drive.appdata) and to read your name and email. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive. The backup contains your routines, activity types, goals, notes, settings, your home screen choices, alarm answers and day reports. You can sign out in the app at any time. To delete the backup, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings > Manage apps, find Dinacharya Launcher and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove the app’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Ads

The free version shows a small banner ad on the dashboard’s Home, Insights and Performance screens, never on the home screen, the list of all apps, the alarm screen, the pause and lock screens or in a PDF. If you choose to, you can also watch short video ads to try Pro features for a while; a video is only ever shown when you tap the button for it. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, interactions with ads and diagnostic information, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other places the app first shows Google’s consent form, and you can change your choice later under Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Google’s own notice is at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Dinacharya Pro removes the ads.

What I do not do

  • I do not sell your information.
  • I do not read your messages, contacts, photos, files or location. The app does not ask for those permissions.
  • The app does not use an accessibility service and does not look inside other apps. A “pause” is a screen in the app’s own home screen, shown before it opens an app you chose; you can always open the app after a few seconds. In Pro it can ask a quick sum, a sentence to type, slow breaths or why you are opening the app; the answers are checked on the phone and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • The optional Strong lock works only after you allow the two permissions it needs, and you can switch it off in Settings. When it is working, a small service on your phone checks which app is in front (through the Usage access you gave) and, only if it is an app you locked or paused, shows the lock or pause screen over it (through “Display over other apps”, which you allow in Android settings). It compares only the app’s name with your own list on the phone and then forgets it. Nothing is saved or sent, and the phone, Settings and system screens are never covered. You can turn it off, or take either permission away, at any time.
  • I do not use your routine or phone-use data for advertising. The ads come from Google and do not use what is inside the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app’s data from the phone, but not a backup you made in your Drive (delete that as described above).

Children

Dinacharya Launcher is not meant for children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.
