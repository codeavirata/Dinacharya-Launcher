APP NAME (max 30)
Dinacharya Launcher

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
A home screen with no apps: just your day, live, and a pause for distractions.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Dinacharya Launcher is a home screen with nothing on it but your day.

A HOME SCREEN WITHOUT APPS
No icons, no folders, no clutter. The whole screen is your day: the time, the date and every slot, with the one you are in highlighted, in four colour themes. It is the live wallpaper that comes with the home screen (Android asks you to confirm it once), so it also shows on your lock screen and in recent apps. Tap your day to open the dashboard.

EVERY APP, ONE SWIPE AWAY
Swipe up for every app, as a plain list with search and an A to Z strip, with the keyboard ready. Swipe down for notifications. Swipe left for the camera, right for the phone.

A PAUSE BEFORE DISTRACTIONS
Choose the apps that pull you away. During focus time they wait a few seconds before they open, so you can go back or carry on. Open anyway is always there. One app is free. Pro adds strict pauses, daily limits, any number of apps and playful pauses: a quick sum, a sentence, slow breaths or saying why.

LOCK APPS FOR A WHILE
Put the apps that eat your time in a group, such as Social, and lock it for the hours you choose, or just for the next hour. A group can also lock while an activity type is on, such as Social during Study, and hide its apps while locked. You can break a lock by waiting half a minute and typing a sentence. One app is free; Pro adds groups, more locks and strict locks that cannot be broken.

STRONG LOCK (OPTIONAL)
Off by default. Locks and pauses then also hold an app opened from recent apps, a notification or another launcher. Needs Usage access and Display over other apps. The phone and Settings are never blocked.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu.

ALARMS THAT ASK, NOT JUST RING
When a slot starts it asks: start now, start after a few minutes, keep going with the last slot, make another plan or take it off. Each slot can ring, vibrate, be a silent notification or nothing. A start-of-day alarm rings until you start the day or put it off, after a line to read and an optional challenge. At bedtime it asks when you are really going to sleep. A test alarm and checklist help alarms ring on time.

TAKE A BREAK, TICK YOUR TO-DOS
Take a break with − and +, or until the slot ends. To-do lists for a slot, which can repeat, show on the wallpaper while it runs.

EDIT YOUR DAY YOUR WAY
Drag to reorder, swipe to delete or add, add notes and activity types (Study, Exercise...). The day always keeps its length. Add routines for weekends or exam days, or one just for tomorrow, and see the day shared by type.

GOALS, SCORE AND REPORTS
Set a daily goal for a slot of your day, with its own to-do list, and keep a streak. Every day gets a score. See today by type and the time in each app and without apps. Weekly trends, suggestions, any day as a PDF.

BACK UP LIKE WHATSAPP
Sign in with Google and back up daily, weekly or monthly to a private space in your own Google Drive. Restore on a new phone in a tap. Nothing is sent to the developer.

FREE, WITH AN OPTIONAL UPGRADE
Dinacharya Launcher is free, with a small ad on three dashboard screens (never on the home screen, alarms, pause or lock screens). Free keeps the home screen, alarms, to-dos and today's report. Dinacharya Pro is Rs 50 a month, or Rs 499 once for life, and removes the ads and unlocks goals, every saved day, the weekly view, deeper analysis, any number of pauses and locks, locked groups, extra routines and all colour themes. Cancel monthly any time. To try first, watch three short videos: everything in Pro opens for a day, then goes back.

PRIVACY
Your routine, answers and reports stay on your phone. The list of your apps and phone-use time are read on the phone only. Backup is optional and goes to your own Drive. No accessibility service and no "see all apps" permission; "display over other apps" is used only if you turn on the strong lock.

You can go back to your old home screen any time in Android's Home app setting.
